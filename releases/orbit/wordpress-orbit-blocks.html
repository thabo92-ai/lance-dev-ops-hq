Make room for Orbit on your device.

Open Orbit and choose Install Orbit to add it to a compatible phone or desktop. The app opens in its own window, with a dedicated icon and a clear reconnect screen. Internet is required for AI replies and saved conversations. Read the quick guide.

Free to use. Clear about costs.

Orbit has no app subscription. Connect your own provider accounts and API keys. Each provider controls its own prices, quotas and availability; consumer chat subscriptions may not include API credit. Your prompts and conversation context are sent to the providers you choose.
